HGT ground elevation uses the source DEM's vertical datum (usually EGM96 for SRTM), not GPS antenna height. Voids remain unavailable in readouts; uncovered areas render flat in the terrain mesh. Display & field help

Globe uses Earth coordinates. Mars/Moon data require their own planetary CRS and are not mixed with terrestrial survey coordinates.
Field checks

GPS: use open sky, inspect the reported accuracy and wait for a fresh fix. Location availability and update frequency are controlled by your browser and phone.

Compass: keep away from magnets and metal; use your device's calibration guidance. Device compass readings may reference magnetic north. GPS course references true north while moving.

This web app cannot reset A-GPS, change Android battery permissions, inspect raw satellites or guarantee recording with the screen locked. Use the existing Own Position page for a connected RTK receiver.

Online basemaps depict the provider's boundaries. For official survey or boundary work, import an authoritative map. Decimal precision is not an accuracy guarantee.

Local storage may be cleared by the browser. Download backups of landmarks and retain original offline map / elevation files.
